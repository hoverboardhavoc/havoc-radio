Havoc Radio wheel UI

Prototype of the menu design for the Si4732 radio's 128×32 OLED, drawn pixel for pixel. Signals are simulated. Reload or use Replay splash to see the start-up screen.

Controls

Turn
Drag the outer ring, scroll over the knob, or ←→
Press
Click the centre, or Enter
Long press
Hold the centre for 0.6 s
Hold + turn
Press the centre and drag around it, or hold Space and use the arrows
Side button
Click it, or B. Its action is set in Settings, Side button.

Navigation rules

  1. Home is the tuning screen. Turning tunes, with no hard band edges.
  2. Press opens the menu. Choosing a band, mode, preset, step or bandwidth returns home.
  3. The left gutter holds only selectable controls. Back (◀) is at the top: turn up past the first entry to reach it. On the main menu the gutter also holds Presets, Mute and Settings. Turning clockwise goes down the entries, then up the gutter to Back, then round to the first entry: one loop around the screen.
  4. A list opens with the cursor on the current value.
  5. Long press: on home it saves a preset, anywhere else it goes home.
  6. Hold + turn on home sets the volume. In a list it jumps a page.
  7. Menus close after 8 s without input (not during text entry). The menu is at most two levels deep.
  8. Text entry: turn through the character ring and press to type. Hold + turn jumps between groups (abc, ABC, 123, symbols, edit). DEL and OK are in the ring. Try Settings, WiFi, then a network.
