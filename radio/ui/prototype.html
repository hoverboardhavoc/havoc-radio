Havoc Radio wheel UI

Prototype of the menu design for the Si4732 radio's 128×32 OLED, drawn pixel for pixel. Signals are simulated; 99.5 MHz on FM sends RDS. Reload or use Replay splash to see the start-up screen.

Controls

Turn
Drag the outer ring, scroll over the knob, or ←→
Press
Click the centre, or Enter
Long press
Hold the centre for 0.6 s
Hold + turn
Press the centre and drag around it, or hold Space and use the arrows
Side button
Click it, or B: back. Hold it, or B, for 1 s: the lamp on or off.

Navigation rules

  1. Home is the tuning screen. Turning tunes, with no hard band edges.
  2. Press opens the menu. Choosing a band, mode, preset, step or bandwidth returns home.
  3. The left gutter holds only selectable controls. Back (◀) is at the top: turn up past the first entry to reach it. On the main menu the gutter also holds Mute. Turning clockwise goes down the entries, then up the gutter to Back, then round to the first entry: one loop around the screen.
  4. A list opens with the cursor on the current value.
  5. Long press: on home it saves a preset, anywhere else it goes home.
  6. Hold + turn on home sets the volume. In a list it jumps a page.
  7. Menus close after 8 s without input (not during text entry or on the RDS Station screen). The menu is at most two levels deep.
  8. On FM the station's RDS name replaces the band and mode on home, and the main menu's Station shows its name, programme type and RadioText. Turning scrolls a long text.
  9. In AM, LSB and USB, Station lists what the frequency guide (EiBi) has within 1 kHz: on air first, marked ▶, then by start time; a press shows an entry's days, language and target. With the clock set, the on-air name replaces the band and mode on home, several taking turns every 3 s. Try 6070 kHz after Simulate NTP sync. Settings, Guide, Update guide simulates the download mode.
  10. Settings, Time: time sync on or off, the NTP server and the age of the last sync. Once the clock is set (Simulate NTP sync, with time sync on and a network joined), home shows the UTC time at the bottom left when the frequency leaves room.
  11. The side button is Back. Holding it for 1 s turns the lamp on and opens the lamp screen, or turns the lamp off. The lamp screen sets the level (1 to 15); a press goes on, SOS, off. SOS runs on its own timer whatever the screen shows. Settings, Light opens the lamp screen.
  12. Text entry: turn through the character ring and press to type. Hold + turn jumps between groups (abc, ABC, 123, symbols, edit). DEL and OK are in the ring. Try Settings, WiFi, then a network.
