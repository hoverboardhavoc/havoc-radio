# The radio's screens for radio/tools/radio_screens.py: home on 6070 kHz, the main menu, the Station screen,
# Settings and each of its pages, and the lamp screen. The masks cover what changes from run to run: the on-air
# name and the signal bars on home's top row, the clock, scanned networks, the age of the last sync, the battery,
# and the guide entries, whose order follows the clock. The lamp is assumed off at the start.
